## Ideas tested
- **F2: tier-1 bundled launch at +4 s, 5-min sale.** In sample spectacular, but an artefact of an unbuyable factory. **Does not hold up** (closed 19 Oct 17:41 UK).
- **Splits for the 1.5x rate** (hit.py, 20 Oct ~16:12 UK, 422 closed, base 39%): creator buy < 0.05 SOL 55%, creator funded ≥ 9.24 SOL 54%, entry MC ≥ $17K 54%, little SOL sold before entry 52%. All still negative on money (−1.8% to −7.1% at 1.5x/−40%). Dropped.
- **8–12 bundle wallets with ≥ 20 SOL** (17:41 study, launches file): 2.3% to $200K on 87, 0% to 10x by 5 min, −21% at 5 min. The exactness of the operator's fingerprint is what mattered (S10).

- 21 Oct 02:30 UK — full diagnosis (all data since 18 Oct, to 21 Oct 01:42 UK; reports/books/diag/S8.md; method: diagnose.py with the family-wise luck check — a filter counts as real only if luck matches it in ≤ 2 of 20 shuffled runs): 582 −19.3% a trade at curve costs; best exit −14.9%; one real cut (entry MC ≥ $16.4K, 146 trades +5.6%, luck 1) but without best only +0.5% and first third −9.3% — break-even, not a book. Factor search with the luck check: nothing real (see above) — with hundreds of trades the test has power, so this is a firm no. No exit or slice makes it break even.
