## Ideas tested
- **The owner's "67% reach 1.5x"** (19 Oct afternoon). Those were the pre-14:17 factory-creator rows. Judged from 14:17 UK, it was 40% reaching 1.5x on 20 closed trades, with 1.5x/−40% at −21.1% (17:41 study). Dropped.
- **Take-profit / stop grid on the 5-s paths** (17:41 study, 17 paths): 1.3x/−40% −23.8%, 1.3x/−30% −17.9%, 1.5x/−25% −8.7%, 2x/−30% −9.5%, 3x/−30% −0.5%, 5x/−40% +0.8% (2 of 17), sell at 60 s −31.6%. A lower target hurts and a tighter stop helps a little. The median 5-min multiple was 0.49, and 41% were under 0.3x at 5 min. Verdict: a lottery book. Dropped.
- **Shadow at 0.05 SOL** (19 Oct 15:57–17:11 UK): 9 legs, −0.183 SOL. Dropped (m039).
- **The launch-file base rate for S1's rule** (13:28 study): 77 launches, 2.6% reached 5x, 5.2% graduated, 5-min +76% mean but median 0.56 and −47% at 15 min (a few outliers). In the 17:41 study it was 79 launches, 0% to $200K, 5-min −63%. No lift.
- **The owner's question on 20 Oct ~16:05 UK: "S1 reaches 1.5x on 42% … common factor?"** The analysis that answered it (hit.py on the 16:11 data, 224 closed): 43% reached 1.5x, and selling at 1.5x / −40% was −14.7% a trade at real costs. The best split was socials: 72% reached 1.5x on 39 (+16.7%), both halves above the base. But socials were known on only 57 of 224 rows at that time, because they were only fetched for coins another book touched. That is selection-biased. This led to v0.18.156 (16:35 UK), which fetches socials for every launch at creation. Other splits (creator has ≥ 22 launches, copy of a coin under $20K) lifted the 1.5x rate but stayed negative. Not adopted as a rule. The socials split is not re-tested on the post-16:35 rows yet.
- **Re-check on data to 21 Oct 00:08 UK:** 128 of 292 reached 1.5x. Median time to 1.5x is 7 s. On the coins that reached 1.5x, the 5-min sale is still −3.9% (the floor sells cost), while a sale at 1.5x would be +19.7%. On all rows, 1.5x/−40% is −14.8%. The entry delay is almost always 0–3 s (271 of 292), so entry timing is not the problem.
