## What it is now
- Code key `sn2` (src/snipe.js). Paper only. **Off the page** (shown in the combined 12-h strip as "S2 proven wallet"). It has never been an executor book.
- Entry: a wallet with a scored record buys ≥ 0.2 SOL within 60 s of launch. The record comes from our own wallets.js scoring: 8+ early buys of ≥ 0.3 SOL, and ≥ 50% of them reached 5x within 24 h. The book buys on that wallet's own trade, at that trade's curve price. Median delay is 12 s, median entry MC ~$3.6K.
- Exit: the study headline is 1.5x/−40% (`x_t15s40`). The off-page strip and the page's real-cost figure use the 5-min sale (`x_h5`) with the curve model and lateFix.
- Started 19 Oct 13:02 UK (v0.18.119). The rule has not changed since.

## Verdict now
Not working. About −15% a trade at 5 min on 1,095 closed rows (21% won, 30% reached 1.5x), and it is negative under every exit in every study. Our "proven" scoring does not pick better coins: the wallet's 5x rate does not order the results.

## Ideas tested
- **Proven wallet in the first 60 s as a launch tell** (13:28 study, launches.csv): 240 launches, 9.6% reached 5x by 5 min (53% ever), 12.5% graduated, 5-min +111% mean but median 0.60. Flagged as **look-ahead risk**, because "proven" is scored on a record that can include these same coins. In the 17:41 study: 196 launches, 0 of them reached $200K, 5-min −26%. No lift.
- **Wallet quality ordering, S6 > S7 > S2** (17:41 study, 91/62/150 closed): 1.5x reached 59% / 42% / 37%; 1.5x/−40% −0.3% / −5.5% / −12.5%. The ordering was established; S2 is the worst of the three.
- **The wallet's own 5x rate inside S2** (17:41 study): it does not order the result. A rate of 0.6–0.7 was best (59% reached 1.5x, n27); 0.7+ was worst (17%, n23). "Our scoring is not measuring what we hoped." Dropped.
- **wallets.csv as a feed** (13:28 study): 2,494 wallets, 37 with a ≥ 60% 5x rate. Buys are only scored once resolved (24 h), so the file cannot say how often these wallets buy now.
