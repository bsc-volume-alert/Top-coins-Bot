## Ideas tested
- **Early inflow as a launch tell** (13:28 study, launches.csv): "15+ SOL from scored buyers within 60 s" was 716 launches, 37% graduated, but bought at the 60-s price only 1.7% reached 5x; +10.7% at 5 min — "the move is over by then". In the 17:41 study, "≥ 15 SOL from scored early buyers in 60 s, bought at 1 min, sold at 5 min" was +0.9% on 552 (16% reached 2x) and −23% by 15 min. Break-even is not a book.
- **Splits for the 1.5x rate** (hit.py, 20 Oct ~16:12 UK, 889 closed, base 37%): fewer sells before entry (`sn_sells` < 7) 52%; fewer trades (< 33) 52%; less SOL sold (< 7.07) 50%; creator best peak ≥ $70K 48%. Every one stayed negative on money (−4.6% to −11.5% at 1.5x/−40%). Dropped.
- **Exits** (17:41 study, 179 closed): every exit −8.9% to −15.7%. No exit rescues it.
