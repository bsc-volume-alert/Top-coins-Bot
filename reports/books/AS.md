## What it is now
- Code key `asoc` (page name AS). A filter on book A's passed rows (`p5e30`, operator-fingerprint coins already removed on the page), not a strategy of its own. Page box only, paper, box stake 0.1 SOL. No executor book (nothing quoted, nothing bought). Code: `src/web.js` `hasSoc`, `devNotOut`, `midWhale`, `stdSet` (lines ~267–275).
- Entry rule = A's trigger plus three filters (the owner's "standard filter set"):
  - A's trigger: 30 SOL bought within 60 s; MC under $100K; coin at least 60 s old; buys ≥ sells and at least 10 buyers in those 60 s; one trade per coin; skip if 70%+ of supply was bought in the launch block, if we never saw the launch, or if 150+ wallets bought in the minute; SOL-paired pools only.
  - 1. Socials: skip only a coin KNOWN to have no socials (`tok_soc` = 0). Unknown (not yet fetched) is kept. 16 of the 83 AS trades to 21 Oct 00:09 UK had socials unknown.
  - 2. Creator not bought-and-sold-out: skip when `dev_hold_entry` = 0 AND the creator's launch buy (`dev_buy_sol`) > 0. A creator who never bought is kept.
  - 3. Biggest buyer in the trigger minute between 3 and 5 SOL: `max_share × sol60` ≥ 3 and < 5 (from `early_json.maxShare` and `early_json.sol60`). No single buyer of 5 SOL or more, and not a thin minute whose biggest buyer is under 3 SOL.
- Exit: `x_flow4`, the runner (same as A): −40% stop until 1.5x; after 1.5x hold while 5-min volume ≥ $100K and buys ≥ sells; 50% trail from the peak; cap 4x. Scored at the page's PumpSwap real-cost model (x/1.02 × 0.967 − 0.016, stops x ≤ 0.62 another −0.05, plus 0.001 SOL a trade fixed fee).
- Started 20 Oct 16:35 UK (v0.18.156, socials-only version). Last rule change 20 Oct 17:45 UK (v0.18.158, standard filters; commit pushed 17:29 UK, the page counts "since the change" from 17:45 UK = `CHG_STD` 16:45 UTC). Page record runs from the price fix (18 Oct 20:25 UK); everything before 17:45 UK is a back-test of a rule chosen by looking at those trades.
