# Successes and failures, coin by coin — 21 Oct 2026 (data to 10:36 UK)

**What this is.** Every book ("coin") in the tracker, with its successful trades grouped and what they had in common, its failures grouped and what they had in common, every idea that was tested to cut the failures, and the proposal already made in reports/latest.md (the plan of 21 Oct 12:30 UK). Nothing here is new analysis: every figure and every statement is transcribed from the book notes in reports/books/ (the 21 Oct 11:00–11:30 UK "revision search" blocks, the earlier 02:30 UK diagnosis, the 08:40 UK S3b, 09:45 UK GR and 10:30 UK S4 blocks, the "Notable coins" lists and the PROPOSAL lines), from reports/books/OFFPAGE.md for the old off-page books, and from the diagnosis files in reports/books/diag/ (whose "best 8 / worst 8" lists use data to 01:42 UK unless stated).

**How to read the words.**
- A trade's result is its **real-cost** return: PumpSwap (pool) costs for graduated coins, the curve cost model for the snipers and PG. "Stopped" = a trade that ended at −35% or worse; "small loser" = −35% to 0; "small winner" = 0 to +50%; "big winner" = +50% or better. "Won %" = share of trades above zero.
- "Without best" (no-best) = the average with the single best trade removed; "without top 3" (no-top3) = with the best three removed. A book that is only positive because of one coin is a lottery, not an edge.
- "Thirds" = the book's trades split into three periods by time; an edge must be positive in all three. "SOL a day at 0.1" = what the book would make per day at 0.1 SOL a trade.
- **Luck scores** ("luck n/20"): the revision search tried every entry factor, every recorded exit and pairs of factors, then ran the same search on 20 sets of shuffled outcomes. "Luck 3/20" means the best rule found on shuffled (meaningless) results was at least as good as the real one in 3 of the 20 runs. 0–2 of 20 = unlikely to be luck; anything higher could easily be luck. A revision counts as **REAL** only if it beats the book in all three thirds, is positive without its best coin, and has luck ≤ 2 of 20 — or has independent support (real-quote legs, a forward record, the same effect on other coins). Everything else is **UNPROVEN**.
- "Back-test" = a rule scored on the trades it was chosen from (flattering). "Forward" / "since the change" = the only real test.
- Two data cuts appear: the books' NUMBERS blocks (data to 10:20 UK) and the revision searches (data to 10:36 UK). Where they differ, both are given with their time.
- Exit names in brackets: runner (x_flow4) = −40% stop until 1.5x, then hold while volume stays strong, 50% trail, cap 4x; 5-min sale (x_h5); 3-min sale (x_h3); 40% trailing stop (x_tsl40); 1.5x-or-−40% (x_t15s40); 2x-or-−40% (x_t2s40); split (x_split); 60-s cut (x_flow4ff); half at 2x then 40% trail (x_h2t40); sell all at 1.5x, no stop (x_tp15); 2x-or-30-min (x_t2n30).
