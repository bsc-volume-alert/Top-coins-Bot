## The six statistics, thresholds and directions (all fixed here, before any count)
1. **R86 (1) — price drift at E.** p_ret = curve price implied by the retention row's state ((vsol/1e9)/(vtok/1e6); the retention row is EXACTLY the evaluator's: event-ordered curve rows, cumulative gross curve buy SOL ≥ 15, or the first curve row for tier ≥ 1). p_t = the same price on the latest received usable (state_ok) curve row with RT ≤ t. ratio = p_t / p_ret. Unknown if the retention row has no valid state (state_ok false) or vsol/vtok missing. **Threshold: ratio < 1 ⇒ veto (skip the E entry).** Bind share = share of E (defined) with ratio < 1. Quartiles of ratio reported.
2. **R86 (2) — same-tape re-buyer ("adder") share at E.** W = the distinct wallets with a curve buy received in (t − 60, t] (the same set whose size is buyers_last_60s; creator included if present). A wallet in W is an ADDER if it has at least one curve buy received on this same tape with RT ≤ t − 60 (it held before the window and bought again inside it). A wallet with two or more buys all inside (t − 60, t] is a REPEAT, not an adder, and is NOT counted — this is the adder/repeat distinction R86 asks for. Distinct from CL-L008's first-time-buyer share, which asked whether a wallet was new to the whole window across mints; this statistic is same-tape only and uses no cross-mint table. share = |adders| / |W|, defined on every E launch (|W| ≥ 5 by construction). **Threshold 0.25: the registered mechanism (adding = conviction) puts the GOOD side at share ≥ 0.25, so the veto side is share < 0.25.** Bind share = share of E with adder share < 0.25 (its complement, ≥ 0.25, is reported beside it; R54 is symmetric). Quartiles reported.
3. **R86 (3) — arrival-regularity CV of the last-60-s buys.** B = received curve buy rows (after dedup, any wallet) with RT in (t − 60, t], in receipt order; n = |B|. **Minimum n = 10** (≥ 9 inter-receipt intervals). CV = population standard deviation ÷ mean of the consecutive RT differences (zero intervals from same-receipt rows are kept). Undefined (unknown at decision) when n < 10 or the mean interval is 0. **Threshold: CV < 0.5 ⇒ veto** (near-regular arrivals; a memoryless arrival process has CV ≈ 1, a fixed ladder CV → 0, 0.5 is the midpoint — a prior, not a fitted value). Two counts, both reported: the minimum-n pre-check (share of E with n ≥ 10) and the bind share on the n ≥ 10 subset AND on all 679 (undefined counted as not vetoed, stated separately). Quartiles of CV and of n reported. R86 rules this is not an R27 activity gate by mechanism but that its estimate depends on the count — hence the two counts.
4. **CL-P1 — sell-absorption share at E.** Over received curve rows with RT in (t − 60, t] (= (created + 240, created + 300]): s = Σ sell SOL ÷ Σ buy SOL (the row's sol field; curve only; pool rows ignored). Undefined if Σ buy SOL = 0. **Threshold: s ≥ 0.5 ⇒ veto.** Bind share and quartiles of s reported; also the share of E with zero sells in the window (so the reader can see how much of the low side is "no sell received", CK21).
5. **CL-P2 — buy-size escalation.** The last 10 received curve buy rows with RT ≤ t whose wallet ≠ the census creator (any age; receipt order = (RT, slot, row_id)). **Minimum n = 10** (fewer ⇒ unknown at decision). ρ = Spearman rank correlation of sol against receipt order (average ranks for ties; if all sizes equal ρ is undefined ⇒ unknown). **Threshold: ρ ≤ −0.5 ⇒ veto** (falling sizes). Minimum-n pre-check share, bind share on the defined subset and on all 679, quartiles of ρ reported.
6. **CL-P3 — creation-clock alignment.** c = create_ts mod 60 (create_ts from tokens_window.csv, integer seconds). **Bin = {58, 59, 0, 1, 2}** (within ± 2 s of a whole minute, 5 of 60 values, uniform expectation 8.33 %). **Veto when c ∈ bin.** Bind share on E reported, plus the full 60-bin distribution over the census (62,789) and over E with a χ² statistic against uniform (59 df; descriptive only — a χ² is not a result and no outcome is involved), and the ± 2 s bin's share vs 5/60 on both populations. The create_ts source is determined empirically and reported: for every launch of the denominator with a received trade whose slot equals create_slot, compare create_ts with that trade's block ts (equal ⇒ create_ts is chain block time) and with floor(recv_ms / 1000) (equal and ≠ block ts ⇒ receipt clock). If create_ts proves to be the tracker's receipt clock, CL-P3 is WITHDRAWN as the f015 proposal says; the counts are still reported as a description of the clock.
