## Additional observations from the review (facts, not disputes)
1. C001's "both fee scenarios" (pool 30 and 125 bps) are vacuous for ID34: all 80 scored positions (35 early + 45 late, both sizes) fill and exit on the curve, so the pool fee never applies — the fee30 and fee125 summaries are identical to the last digit. The curve fee (125 bps) is a constant in eval-1.1. Codex did not claim otherwise; worth stating so nobody reads the two scenarios as a sensitivity.
2. C001 denominator arithmetic: 4,913 streamed − 301 never-retained − 974 outage-excluded = 3,638, which equals the queue's development N under the six-interval ledger.
3. C002 completed-curve entry, checked on the raw rows (regenerated target_rows.json): for all four mints the first and only curve row is one 85.005359057-SOL buy that leaves vtok exactly at the 279,900,000,000,000 completion floor, i.e. the curve was already complete at the first observed trade; eval-1.1's state_ok still accepts that state for a buy because its price (vsol/vtok) is below CURVE_MAX_PX × 1.1. The 2-h exits are scored on pumpswap rows carrying quote_mint = wSOL, base_dec 6, quote_dec 9, dec_ver ps-0.19.9 (evidence present), but the states read vsol 5,429 SOL / vtok 3.24 M tokens (LLzYn4ZZ exit), 5,088 / 3.45 M (dEJEFGBe), 5,427 / 3.24 M (fBidsBVN) and 1,160 SOL / 14.96 M tokens (5kdsd8zW at 30 s). A fresh pump.fun migration carries roughly 79–85 SOL and ~207 M tokens, so these states imply 14–65× more SOL and 14–65× fewer tokens than a migration can hold; the implied price is 157–4,070× the curve-completion price. This is consistent with Codex's "unit/decoder" caveat and cannot be settled here without chain access (none used). For the three LLzYn/dEJEF/fBids mints the pool rows have evidence fields only from ts 1794045488 onward (≈ 2 h after creation); their first 1.8–2 h of pool rows carry no evidence and are correctly unavailable.
4. C003/C004 artifacts are deterministic and stdlib-only; both reproduce bit-for-bit in under a minute. C003's seven evidence-branch commits still resolve on origin to the same SHAs (ls-remote, 11 Nov 23:10 UTC); contents not read.
