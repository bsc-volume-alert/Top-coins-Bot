### 3.1 CL-P3 descriptive counts (the premise check the f015 proposal asked for)
create_ts source: for 3,630 of the 3,638 denominator launches a received trade sits in the creation slot (create_slot) and create_ts equals that trade's block ts in 3,630 / 3,630; the other 8 have no row in the creation slot. create_ts is therefore the CHAIN BLOCK TIME, not the tracker's receipt clock — the withdrawal clause of the f015 proposal is not triggered. Distribution of create_ts mod 60: census (62,789 launches) χ² = 63.37 on 59 df (p ≈ 0.33 — consistent with uniform), largest second-bin 1,157 / smallest 979 against an expectation of 1,046; the ± 2 s bin holds 5,068 = 8.07 % against 8.33 % expected (exact binomial p ≈ 0.018 — a slight DEFICIT, not a spike). Denominator (3,638): χ² = 66.20 (p ≈ 0.24); bin 293 = 8.05 % (p ≈ 0.57). E (679): χ² = 63.53 (p ≈ 0.32); bin 68 = 10.0 % (p ≈ 0.13). So the hypothesis' premise — scripted launchers producing a spike at the whole minute — is not visible in the creation clock of this census at second resolution: the distribution is flat. The veto would bind on 10 % of E (inside the band) but it would be selecting a slice of a uniform distribution. This is a count, not an outcome; the proposal's status is the coordinator's call (my recommendation is in §5).

## 4. Reading the record (no verdicts; what the counts say about testability)
- R86 (1), R86 (2), CL-P2 and CL-P3 bind inside 5–95 % on E with their pre-fixed thresholds, in both halves and in every cell, with ≤ 4 unknown-at-decision launches each. They are TESTABLE in R54's sense on data of this shape. Whether they bind on a released window is unknown (the shares above are for this window only).
- R86 (3) does NOT bind at CV < 0.5: the minimum observed CV on the 596 defined launches is 0.56 and 90 % are above 1.0. Inter-receipt intervals of curve buys in the last 60 s before a 5-min E decision are over-dispersed (bursty), not regular — in part because the tracker's receipt clock batches rows (same recv_ms ⇒ zero intervals beside long ones), so "regular bot ladder" is not what the receipt clock shows even when it exists on-chain. Recorded "unlikely to bind" and held; no second threshold here (R41 / R91). On a released window the proposal would need a different statistic or a re-registered threshold — stated now, not chosen now.
- CL-P1 binds on 88.4 % — inside the band, but a veto that removes seven launches in eight leaves 79 E launches to trade on, and the kept side would have fewer than 30 resolved trades in some cells by construction (B kept = 19 launches). The median E launch has sells ≈ buys in SOL over its last minute (s median 1.03, q1 0.73): the prior "half the inflow leaving" is simply the normal state of a 5-min E tape on this window. Recorded "can bind" because R54 is satisfied; whether 0.5 is the threshold to carry to a released window is a question for the register, not for this window (R41). Only 11 of 679 E launches had zero sells in the window, so the CK21 inactivity confound is small for this statistic.
- CL-P3 binds on 10 % but its premise fails descriptively (§3.1).
