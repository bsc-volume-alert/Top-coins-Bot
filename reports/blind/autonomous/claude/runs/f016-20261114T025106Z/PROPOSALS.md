## CL-P4 — Depth-normalised net inflow at E
Hypothesis: the same 10 SOL of net buying means something different on a curve holding 35 SOL of virtual reserve than on one holding 80 SOL; what predicts whether the E entry's 30 % trail survives its first minute is net inflow RELATIVE to curve depth, not the raw SOL. Statistic: d = (Σ buy SOL − Σ sell SOL over received curve rows in the last 60 s) ÷ (vsol / 1e9 of the latest received valid curve state at t). Veto when d < 0.02 (prior: 2 % of the reserve per minute is the rate at which the trail's own 30 % band would be eaten in a quarter of an hour of symmetric flow; not fitted). Why it might work: F006 measured raw net_inflow_last_60s for the detection half only and C009 measured curve progress as a liquidity description; neither normalised flow by depth, and the ratio is dimensionless across curve stages. Knowable-at-time: both inputs are on received rows with RT ≤ t. Distinct from CL-P1 (a sell ÷ buy ratio, no depth) and from R27's activity count.

## CL-P5 — Buy-concentration entropy of the last-60-s tape
Hypothesis: E requires ≥ 5 distinct buyers in the last minute, but a tape where 5 wallets place 40 buys is a ladder farm and one where 20 wallets place 40 buys is a crowd; the normalised Shannon entropy H = −Σ p_w log p_w ÷ log n_wallets of the per-wallet share of buy ROWS in the last 60 s separates them regardless of the count. Veto when H < 0.7 (prior: 0.7 is roughly the entropy of a tape where one wallet places a third of the rows and the rest are spread evenly; not fitted). Why it might work: CL-L002 asked about size dispersion and found nothing; F006's HHI was of HOLDINGS at 5 min for detection; this is concentration of ACTIVITY, independent of size and of how much is held, and a farm that varies its clip sizes to defeat CL-L002 still needs its few wallets to place the rows. Knowable-at-time: received rows only. Needs the R86 (3)-style minimum-n pre-check (n_buys60 ≥ 10; 87.8 % of E qualify on the development window per this run's count).

## CL-P6 — Up-tick share of the pre-decision price path (unconventional)
Hypothesis: a price path that is TOO smooth before the decision — almost every received valid curve state at or above the previous one — is a scripted push, not demand finding a price; organic tapes have down-ticks from early sellers being absorbed. Statistic: u = share of consecutive pairs of received valid curve states in the last 60 s with price_i ≥ price_{i−1}. Veto when u > 0.9 (prior; not fitted). Why it might work: it is a path-roughness statistic, orthogonal to the level statistics already registered (R86 (1) compares two prices; CL-P1 compares two flows) and to counts; a farm that produces regular sizes and irregular receipts (which this run found defeats R86 (3)) still produces a monotone price path if it never sells. Knowable-at-time: states on received rows with RT ≤ t. Caveat to state in the PROTOCOL: with sells ≈ buys on the median E tape (CL-P1 count: s median 1.03), monotone paths may be rare and the bind share must be pre-checked; a down-tick is also produced by any sell, so u is partly a "no sell received" statistic and CK21's activity pre-check applies.
