Three new ideas this run. (1) Launch crowding (unconventional, market-structure rather than coin-level): a live coin reaching 5 min while few other launches were retained in the previous 10 min should keep its buyers. (2) First-time-buyer share: require at least 80 % of last-60-s buyers to have never appeared on the retained tape in the window — fresh demand rather than the rotating cohort. (3) Slot diversity: require the last-60-s buys to land in at least 8 distinct slots, i.e. spread in time rather than bundled, as a bot-bundle screen. Only (1) was tested, registered @ 3a281db with a fixed threshold of 10 before I looked at the crowd distribution. The window turned out busier than my prior (quartiles 14/20/28 other launches per 10 min), so the rule bound on only 68 launches; a post-hoc quartile split shows no pattern either. Quiet periods look like periods with fewer buyers to sell to, not less competition. The control reproduced f006p2 exactly (679 / 419 / −10.011572).
