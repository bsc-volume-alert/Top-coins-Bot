WHAT THIS IS
A pump.fun launch research bot. Every pump.fun launch is recorded (~37,000 a day). Selected launches are examined at 60/90/120 s after creation; 22 simulated "books" (entry rules) decide at those checks; each entry is filled by a simulator against the real curve/pool reserves (0.2 SOL position plus a parallel 0.02 SOL position), exits on a trailing stop (40% off the high on the curve, 25% after migration) or at a 2-hour horizon. Live trading is OFF. Every result in the data is simulated unless the file is live_trades.
Headline so far: every book loses. On the clean window (since the measurement fix, 09:46 UTC): 579 closed positions, median −79% at 0.02 SOL after fees, 9% winners, typical loser closes 54 s after entry, typical winner is held 15 min. One large win today (The 7 Figure Fish, 3nPBoqms…, 10.65× high, exited 6.33× on the trail) is one shared opportunity entered by 14 books at the same second — not 14 wins. No feature has survived a proper test; the "factory-funder" group looked less bad but the effect vanishes without its best coin.

THE AGREED WAY TO ANALYSE IT (Claude + Codex)
1. The unit is the COIN, not the book. The 22 books overlap heavily (e.g. every FDB position is also an A1 position at the same second); analysing book by book counts the same market event many times. Build one timeline per mint: what was knowable at 15/30/60/90/120 s; each book's eligibility/decision/entry/exit; the price path AFTER our exit (token_life, 24-h peak); migration and $100K as separate, detected (not verified) outcomes. Keep every launch, including the ones no book entered — the failures are the denominator.
2. Measure opportunity from an ACTIONABLE entry, in multiples of the 60-s price (2×, 3×, 5× within 2 h; +200% = 3×), with time to the high, worst drawdown before the high, and the after-cost result under FIXED exit policies — never peak market cap alone, never "percent" loosely. Keep gross multiples separate from after-fee returns (the 0.02 position pays a fixed 0.0015 SOL fee = 7.5% on top of impact).
3. Group paths, numerical boundaries fixed before looking at forward data: immediate collapse / small rise then collapse / large run then collapse / sustained run / quiet start then later run (−80% from the high = collapse).
4. Compare entry-time features between runners and FAILURES OF THE SAME AGE, CAP AND NET BUYING: buyer arrival and retention, concentration, creator/bundle selling, funding provenance (a label, not a verified identity), liquidity, farm-wallet share. Use only information available BEFORE the decision (the farm_* fields inside each check row are knowledge-time; farm_dumps/farm_wallets are event-time tables). A signal visible only after the collapse starts is not a warning.
5. Then score the 22 books as selection/exit policies on the same coins: coverage, fills, missing outcomes (unknown ≠ zero), after-cost returns, severe losses (≤ −50%), result without the best mint, exposure; paired comparisons on shared mints; uncertainty by bootstrapping MINTS, rows sharing one decision count once.
6. Exits: apply the same predefined policies to the whole cohort (the 7 Figure Fish chart must not pick the exit). For positions that reached 2× and still closed at a loss, reconcile trigger vs landing from the recorded reserves before calling it a dump.
7. Nothing becomes a trading rule from this data alone: the forward plan (forward_plan_6nov.txt) pre-registers hypotheses, thresholds, outcomes and a stop rule; launches after 14:22:20 UTC are reserved for testing, with a running three-arm experiment (E60 / E90 / E90C) isolating whether waiting until 90 s helps.

KNOWN LIMITS: no external outcome source succeeded (pump.fun 403; GMGN is a current ranking); trade streams exist only for entered coins and only to 125 min; before today only the 60-s checkpoint existed for unexamined launches; the historical "214 winners" material has no contemporaneous failure cohort; two under-checked windows (09:47–10:25, 11:06–11:45 UTC) and several 5-minute dropped check passes since 14:23 UTC; the 0.02 position has no independent exit (it exits when the 0.2 position's trail fires).
