MEASUREMENT (fixed)
  Simulated executable fills, fill-2 policy (no stale fallback; data failures are rows, not zero). Position size for the headline: 0.02 SOL; r = exit multiple (0.02 proceeds at the book's exit) − 1 − 0.075 (0.0015 SOL fee). Both the pre-fee multiple and the fee are exported.
  The "0.02 proceeds at the 0.2 book's exit" column is what is measured; an independent 0.02 strategy does not exist yet and nothing here claims one.
  Follow-up: a position counts as completed only when closed; the 2-h horizon applies; "equal follow-up" = only launches whose entry is ≥ 2 h before the read.
  Uncertainty: bootstrap over MINTS (not rows); rows sharing same_decision_key are one observation; sensitivity by funder group where ≥ 10 groups exist.

PRIMARY OUTCOMES (per book, forward set, equal follow-up)
  COMMON DENOMINATOR: eligibility is defined ONCE, at the 60-s check, on the BL population (screened launches with net ≥ 15 SOL and mc ≥ 60 SOL at 60 s). Every eligible mint stays in every arm's and every book's accounting, whether or not that arm/book entered it. For H1, BL and FDL are both measured over this same denominator (FDL's funding pass is then a subset), so the gate's opportunity cost is measured, not hidden.
  O1 per eligible launch: sum of r over COMPLETED trades ÷ eligible launches, reported WITH the categories separately: deliberate no-entry (0 trading P&L by construction), skipped-dump guard (0), failed fill / data failure (UNKNOWN — not zero: shown as a count and as a sensitivity band, best case 0 and worst case −1 − fee per attempt), open (unknown until closed).
  O2 per filled trade: mean and median r over completed trades; win rate; severe-loss rate (r ≤ −0.5); result without the best mint.
  O3 coverage: of the registry's runners (migrated / ≥ $100K kept separate, detected not verified) launched in the period, the share the book checked, qualified, filled; missed runners listed with their terminal reason.
  O4 exposure: positions open concurrently (max, mean) and SOL at risk at 0.02 a position.

HYPOTHESES (each with the threshold that would count as support; none is a trading rule until confirmed on the confirmation period)
  H1 (funding gate): BL (no funding test, 15-SOL screen) vs FDL (same bar + funding pass) — the funding pass does NOT improve O1. Support for "gate hurts": BL's O1 ≥ FDL's O1 with the mint-bootstrap 95% interval excluding 0 for the difference. Support for "gate helps": the reverse.
  H2 (high-count funders): among BL fills, grp = high-count has O2 mean > the rest. Threshold: difference > 0 with interval excluding 0 AND the result without the best mint still > 0. Label: source heuristic, not verified identity.
  H3 (2×-then-lost): for completed trades that reached 2× before exit and closed ≤ 0, reconcile trigger vs landing from the recorded reserves (s_res_trig vs s_res_exit), the transaction slots in trades_books, and the timestamps. A landing valuation < 50% of the trigger valuation establishes a large RECORDED GAP only; whether it is market movement, observation delay or a valuation defect is decided per case from slots + coherent reserve states + timestamps. No exit-policy conclusion is drawn from the gap size alone.
  H4 (90-s gate, PROPOSED experiment, not yet built; owner's go required for the load): three arms on the common denominator above, identical sizing/execution/exits — E60 enters at the 60-s check; E90 enters at the 90-s check if the SAME bar (net ≥ 15, mc ≥ 60 SOL) still holds at 90 s (an eligible mint that fails at 90 is a deliberate no-entry, kept in the denominator); E90C = E90 plus net90 − net60 > +2 SOL and nothing else. Reuses the existing 60/90-s check metrics (no extra Helius calls); valuation calls bounded so the experiment cannot block collection. Prospective data begins at the stamped activation time of the experiment, never retrospectively at 14:22. Analysis PAIRED BY MINT: differences between arms estimated by resampling the same mints across arms. Primary O1, secondary O2–O4.
  H5 (sells share): EXPLORATORY, not a frozen policy — no threshold is specified here. If a specification is wanted it must be trained on the discovery set and frozen in a dated amendment before the forward read; until then nothing about sells share is tested on forward data, and Codex's small negative result is noted as not establishing that it never helps.
